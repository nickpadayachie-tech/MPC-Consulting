Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a website cost?

Website packages start from R249 per month for hosting and maintenance, with a once-off setup fee from R1500. See our website packages above for full details, or request a free consultation for a quote tailored to your business.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most business websites are designed and launched within a few weeks, depending on the number of pages, how ready your content is and how quickly you provide feedback. We give you a clear timeline after our initial consultation.

Do you set up business email and Microsoft Outlook?

Yes. We set up professional business email accounts on your domain and help you configure them in Microsoft Outlook and on mobile devices so you can send and receive mail from your own business address.

Do you help with domain registration and website hosting?

Yes. We assist with .co.za domain registration and arrange reliable website hosting, SSL certificates and the technical setup so your site is secure and online.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Absolutely. Every website we build is fully responsive and tested across mobile, tablet and desktop, so your customers get a fast, clean experience on any screen.

Can you maintain my website after it goes live?

Yes. Our monthly packages include ongoing maintenance such as updates, security and backups, so your website stays secure, fast and up to date.
